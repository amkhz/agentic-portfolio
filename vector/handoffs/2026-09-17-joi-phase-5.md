## Gotchas learned

- **Asking Justin for "the open question" returns team posture, not the
  question.** Two prompts aimed at the unresolved item both came back as "we
  stay flexible" / "support and sales hold it until wider rollout." That is the
  voice; do not re-prompt hoping for the abstract category.
- **Spaced-hyphen dashes came back three more times in dictation.** The ban is
  unchanged (restructure, never respell), but expect them in every raw sample
  and never treat them as style.
- **Feel is the verdict verb.** Seven "felt right / didn't feel right / made me
  feel" in nine samples. The draft's "The decision:" and "The move was
  noticing" are the single most visible tell in #230.
- **`git checkout -b X origin/main`** failed with "Needed a single revision" in
  this checkout even after a fetch; `main` and `origin/main` resolved to the
  same SHA, so branching off local `main` was equivalent. Not investigated.
